Why we exist

The next great failure won't begin with a missile. It will begin with infrastructure that can't survive its own complexity.

Satellites, grids and autonomous machines now act at software speed. We build the layer that keeps them inside their limits, and proves it.

The platform

USG Radius sits between intent and action.

One foundation for machines, AI and software. Before anything acts, USG Radius checks that it is allowed, and keeps a signed record of what happened.

  1. IntentA person, a model or a machine asks to do something.
  2. CheckThe request is tested against defined limits before it runs.
  3. ActionOnly what passes reaches the hardware or the system.
  4. RecordEvery decision is signed, so anyone can check it later.
